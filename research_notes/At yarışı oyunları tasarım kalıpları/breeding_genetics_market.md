*Source note for the report writer (14.10.2026):* every full-page fetch failed in this environment (DNS/egress blocked for rivalstarshorseracing.com, thirdtimegames.com, photofinish.live, medium.com, fandom.com, prydwen.gg, uma.guide, pocketgamer.com). ## Q1. Who can breed, cooldowns, gestation/maturity, per-horse limits

### Takeaway
Successful games almost never allow unlimited breeding. They gate it with one or more of these: a lifetime "slot" budget (ZED Champions: 5 − generation), monthly caps that differ by sex (ZED RUN: stallions 3/month, mares 1/month), seasonal breeding windows (Photo Finish: days 8–14 of a season), mare rest periods (Howrse: 10 months), facility capacity (Rival Stars: 3 foaling stalls), and "must retire from racing first" rules (Photo Finish, ZED Champions). Access to stallions comes from three sources: your own, NPC/game studs (Rival Stars' hourly rotating 5, Starters Orders' AI mares), and a player stud market with fees.

- The breeding menu shows your mares plus five stallions: four game-generated studs refreshed every hour, plus one of your own stallions that you nominated as stud. A player does not need to own a stallion. — [Rival Stars fan wiki: Breeding](https://rival-star-horse-racing-player-guide.fandom.com/wiki/Breeding); [Mobile Update 1.39 – Breeding 2.0 (Nov 2022)](https://rivalstarshorseracing.com/2022/11/30/rival-stars-horse-racing-breeding-2-0-update-1-39/)

- There are 3 foaling stalls. One is required and built through the Story Chapters; the other two are optional and unlocked with Gold. This caps how many foals can be in progress at once. — [fan wiki: Foaling stall](https://rival-star-horse-racing-player-guide.fandom.com/wiki/Foaling_stall)

### Inferences
- Limits come in three main shapes:
  - **Lifetime budget**: ZED Champions slots, Howrse donkeys.
  - **Rate cap**: ZED RUN monthly caps, Howrse 10-month mare rest, Photo Finish's one-week window per season.
  - **Capacity**: Rival Stars stalls.

  The lifetime budget is the only one that bounds total supply. The other two only slow it down.
- Sex asymmetry (stallions 3×, mares 1×) copies real-world biology and makes good mares the scarce input, which in turn supports a mare market.
- "Retire before breeding" (Photo Finish, ZED Champions) turns breeding into a horse's second career. It also stops a horse from racing and breeding at once.
- At Sevdalısı's current `config/genetics.config.json` (`breedingCooldownDays: 180`, 36–180 months breeding age) is a pure rate cap. By the evidence above it would still let supply grow without bound unless it is paired with a lifetime slot budget or a retirement requirement.

- I found no per-season cap on how many mares a Photo Finish stud can cover.
- No reliable source found for the *Horse Racing Manager* series (any edition). Treat it as uncovered.

- Current Umamusume daily rental cap: no number found.

## Q2. Genetics model: stat inheritance, potential, hidden genes, coat, pedigree, inbreeding, nicks

### Takeaway
There are two families of design:
- **"Parent average + variance + cap"** (Rival Stars, Howrse, Starters Orders). Simple to explain, but it generates complaints about variance.
- **"Pedigree theory"** (Winning Post, Derby Stallion), where named combinations of sire line and dam-sire line ("nicks") and controlled inbreeding add a hidden "explosive power" bonus. Inbreeding trades that bonus against explicit health and temperament risk, and parent health can reduce the risk.

Photo Finish adds a strong anti-exploit idea: stats are hidden until retirement, and a paid "breeding report" sells probabilistic information.

### Cited Findings
**Rival Stars**
- In-game guidance says foal base stats are most likely to average the parents' stats. Players report substantial variance, often a couple of letter grades off. — [Steam: "Breeding stats are terrible"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1166860/discussions/0/4626979614631196639/); [Steam: New Breeding Guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1166860/discussions/0/6450905019301401493/)
- Update 1.44 ("Better Breeding"): foals bred on 1.44 will on average have base stats closer to their parents' than before. The developer tightened variance after complaints. — [Mobile Update 1.44 – Better Breeding](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/post/mobile-update-1-44-better-breeding)
- Players report that a foal's star grade cannot exceed its parents' grade. Mixed-grade parents still give good stats as long as the foal stays at or below the lower parent's grade. Grades run 1–10 stars; stat levels run 1–50 within a grade. — [Steam: Foal Stall Levels](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1166860/discussions/0/4407417406449711068/) (player reports)
- Crossbreeding: a horse with less than 90% of any one breed counts as a crossbreed. Breeding back above 90% of one breed makes it purebred again. Foal breed share is always an even split of the parents' makeup (two purebreds give 50/50), shown on the "Definite Breed Makeup" screen. A horse can carry at most 4 breeds. Breed-specific stat bonuses can be inherited. — [Breeding Guide – Crossbreeding and Traits](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/breeding-guide---crossbreeding-and-traits); [Mobile Update 1.53](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/post/mobile-update-1-53-crossbreeding-and-traits); [PikPok FAQ: Selle Français](https://faq.pikpok.com/hc/en-nz/articles/21639455226905-Selle-Fran%C3%A7ais)
- Traits:
  - A foal gets most of its traits from its parents plus **at least one from a Random Trait Pool**.
  - Traits both parents share are more likely to pass on.
  - A foal never gets the same trait twice.
  - A Caretaker can swap one foal trait for a random one the foal did not inherit.
  - Star Club and Exotic traits come only from specific sources.

  — [Breeding Guide – Crossbreeding and Traits](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/breeding-guide---crossbreeding-and-traits); [Rivalstars fan wiki: Breeding Guide](https://rivalstars-horseracing.fandom.com/wiki/Breeding_Guide)
- Coat: white and black are the rarest colors, and breeding them together raises the odds of either. Dilution genes can produce a double dilution even when neither parent shows one. — [Rival Stars Breeding Guide](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/breeding-guide)
- A 2020 review: foals inherit markings, track preferences and a rough idea of coat color, but "the game is not an accurate genetics simulation." — [The Mane Quest review (2020, older)](https://themanequest.com/blog/2020/6/21/review-rival-stars-horse-racing-desktop-edition-gorgeous-graphics-and-forgivable-flaws)

**Photo Finish LIVE**
- The genetics system pairs "hundreds of genes" for each foal; genes affect running ability, looks, preferred tracks and aging. Each horse carries two gene sets, one from each parent; each parent passes half its genes. — [Beginner's Guide to Breeding](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=5662); [Photo Finish 2 whitepaper: Genetics-Based Breeding](https://thirdtimedev.gitbook.io/photo-finish-2-nft-horse-racing-whitepaper/upcoming-plans/genetics-based-breeding)
- The developer states that no attributes, preferences or items directly affect breeding outcomes. There are no "breeding boost" items. — [Third Time: Beginner's Guide to Photo Finish](https://thirdtimegames.com/draft-a-beginners-guide-to-photo-finish-live-part-1/)
- Grades run D to SSS with sub-grades (S−, S, S+); each grade is a range. Six attributes (Start, Speed, Stamina, Finish, Heart, Temper) are graded but **revealed only on retirement**; the overall grade is their average. Three preferences: Surface (Dirt/Turf), Direction (Left/Right), Condition (Firm/Soft). — [Third Time: Beginner's Guide to Photo Finish](https://thirdtimegames.com/draft-a-beginners-guide-to-photo-finish-live-part-1/); [games.gg: Photo Finish Live](https://games.gg/photo-finish-live/)
- Inbreeding penalty: parents cannot be shared going back four generations, or a penalty applies. — [Third Time: Beginner's Guide to Breeding](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=5662); also [Horse Racing Capital: How to Breed in PFL](https://medium.com/@horse-racing-capital/how-to-breed-in-photo-finish-live-f62be468186) (third-party)
- Paid "Breeding Reports" preview likely foal outcomes (including grade ranges) for one stud × mare pair, at 4,000 DERBY (about $50). Owners of 20 Photo Finish PFPs get some free reports per mare per season. — [Beginner's Guide to Breeding](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=5662); [Horse Racing Capital: Guide to Breeding Reports](https://medium.com/@horse-racing-capital/a-guide-to-photo-finish-live-breeding-reports-3d8a52031507)

**ZED RUN (original, older)**
- Four bloodlines, rarest first: Nakamoto, Szabo, Finney, Buterin. The genesis supply was planned at 38,000 horses released periodically. — [TDN: Breeding & Selling the Million-Dollar Racehorse That Doesn't Exist](https://www.thoroughbreddailynews.com/the-week-in-review-breeding-selling-the-million-dollar-racehorse-that-doesnt-exist/)
- A foal's Z-number is the sum of its parents' Z-numbers (Z9 × Z8 gives Z17). The range is Z1–Z268, and lower means "purer." Breeding therefore raises Z, so purity decays every generation. — [MomentRanks breeding tutorial](https://momentranks.wpcomstaging.com/2021/07/02/zed-run-breeding-tutorial-breeding-charts-and-basic-strategy/); [ZED RUN for Dummies](https://medium.com/sidechain/zed-run-for-dummies-d99007c87cdd)
- A third-party article claims bloodline carried 80% of the weighting in a horse's ability rating. This is unverified and from the ETH era. — [Zombie Racing horse guide](https://zombie.racing/horse-guide/)

**Winning Post 10 (Koei Tecmo; 2025/2026 editions)**
- Official manual: inbreeding can bring out an ancestor's traits, but it raises the risk of temperament problems (気性難) and weakened constitution (体質弱化). The closer the inbreeding, the larger both the benefit and the risk. — [WP10 2026 manual: 配合理論](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/manual/WP102026/jp/7300.html); [WP10 2025 manual](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/manual/WP102025/jp/7300.html)
- Nicks (sire line × dam-sire line) add hidden "explosive power" (爆発力): single nick +2, double +4, triple +6, quadruple +8. — [sokuniv: WP10 配合理論](https://sokuniv.com/winningpost-10-seeding/)
- Inbreeding explosive power by blood share: 12.5% (4×4) +1, 18.75% (3×4) +3, 25% (3×3) +4, ≥31.25% +5, plus +3 for each speed factor on the duplicated ancestor. — [Alnas Games: WP10 配合理論](https://alnasgames.com/wp10/breeding/haigouriron.html). WP9 2021 used different numbers (12.5% with 2 speed factors gave +7). — [kamioka-games: WP9 2021 inbreeding](https://kamioka-games.com/4170/)
- If total inbreeding blood share exceeds 25%, explosive power is halved. — [note.com 検証勢: WP10 2025 配合理論](https://note.com/kensyouzei/n/n7928dc93006a)
- Inbreeding risk (危険度) by blood share: 12.5% gives 1, 18.75% gives 2, 25% gives 3, ≥31.25% gives 4. Parent health reduces it: both parents health S or better −2, both A or better −1, and one healthy parent alone does nothing. Maternal-line inbreeding carries 1 less risk. — [kamioka-games: WP10 inbreeding](https://kamioka-games.com/9102/); [ps4.fcf.tokyo: maternal inbreeding](https://ps4.fcf.tokyo/winning-post-10/maternal-inbreed/)
- Line-breeding where sire and dam share a parent line but differ in sub-line gives +2, and +3 more if both are SP lines (WP10 2025). — [note.com: WP10 2025](https://note.com/kensyouzei/n/n7928dc93006a)
- Fan analyses openly say they are incomplete: "完全版ではなく、不明な点が残っている" ("not complete; unknowns remain"). The formulas are hidden and reverse-engineered by the community. — [winningpostinfo.com](https://www.winningpostinfo.com/archives/6151)

**Derby Stallion (Dabisuta 2 for Switch 2, 2026; Dabimasu mobile)**
- Nicks are judged from sire line × dam-sire line compatibility and shown as ★1–★3. They are a separate mechanism from outbreeding. — [keiba-bloodline: ダビスタ2配合理論まとめ](https://keiba-bloodline.com/game/?p=1397)
- In Dabimasu, nicks lift overall foal ability with *no* downside, unlike inbreeding, which brings leg weakness (脚部不安) and temperament problems (気性難). One site says a higher-ability mare gives a bigger nick effect. — [ダビマス: ニックス・アウトブリード](https://xn--zckmb0f5346anjua.com/combination/nixoutbreeding.php)
- Named tiers of pedigree combination exist: 凝った配合 / 見事な配合 / 完璧な配合 ("elaborate / splendid / perfect"). A higher total cross percentage scores higher, but "deep crosses" carry risk. — [keiba-bloodline](https://keiba-bloodline.com/game/?p=1397); [piyotakejapan: Epiphaneia route](https://piyotakejapan.com/47384)
- A pre-release analysis said Dabisuta 2 strengthens nicks and adds mare-line inbreeding (牝馬クロス). This is pre-release analysis, not verified post-release. — [note.com おぐQ: pre-release analysis](https://note.com/easy_hippo6138/n/n7fcd259347fb)

**Howrse**
- BLUP (Best Linear Unbiased Prediction) is a genetic index from −100 to +100. Parents' BLUP affects the foal's genetic potential (GP) and skills at birth, and **GP cannot increase after birth**. — [Howrse official breeder manual: Genetics](https://www.howrse.com/aide/manuelEleveur/?type=section-4-2)
- Fan sources say:
  - BLUP comes from the training level of the horse's 3 best skills, its competition wins and its age, and it rises until age 10. — [Howrse fan wiki: BLUP](https://howrse.fandom.com/wiki/BLUP)
  - If the parents' average BLUP is below zero, the foal is born with no inborn skills. — [The Ultimate Howrse Helper: BLUP](https://theultimatehowrsehelper.weebly.com/blup.html)
  - Estimated inborn skills = (GP father + GP mother) × 10% ± 4% when parent BLUP is 100. — [The Book of Howrse: Calculating Skills & GPs](https://thebookofhowrse.weebly.com/calculating-skills--gps.html)

  All of these are older fan content.
- The official app listing frames the core loop as "develop the genetic potential of future foals by choosing the best stallions, and analyze the parents' innate skills and BLUP." Community tools estimate the odds of coat colors in foals. — [Howrse on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id684830457)

**Starters Orders 7**
- Players judge gamebred stallions by inspecting each foal's "green bars" (potential, extra speed, cruising burst). A stallion's potential matters more than a mare's: a half-potential mare with a full-potential stud can produce great foals. Good AI mares are rare. — [SO forum: How to Breed Successfully in SO7](http://www.startersorders.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?f=19&t=20820); [SO forum: Guide to Flat Breeding](http://startersorders.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?f=16&t=7962)

**Umamusume (inheritance analog)**
- Spark types:
  - Blue raises base stats.
  - Pink raises distance/track aptitude.
  - Green passes on unique skills (3★ units only).
  - White passes on normal skills.

  Inheritance happens at the start of the run and at two later in-run events. — [Deltia's Gaming: Sparks Guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281251); [Goonhammer: Understanding Sparks](https://www.goonhammer.com/212413-2)
- Blue Sparks inherit at 70/80/90% for 1/2/3★. Pink, Race and White rates are much lower: 1–5% or 2–5%, depending on the guide (sources conflict). Affinity multiplies the base rate by (1 + affinity/100), and each parent and grandparent has its own affinity, so grandparent rates are roughly halved. Example: a 3★ White Spark at 30 affinity is about 12% instead of 9%. — [uma.guide: Parenting](https://uma.guide/guides/parenting-in-umamusume); [Uma Reference: Chance of Inheriting Sparks](https://www.umareference.com/guide/legacies/chance-of-inheriting-sparks)
- Affinity has three ranks: △ (bad), ○ (good), ◎ (great). — [GameRant: Legacy Affinity Guide](https://gamerant.com/umamusume-legacy-affinity-guide/)
- A 6☆ total of inherited red/pink sparks can turn a spark into a "Gene" spark (e.g. Gene of Long Distance or Turf); 12☆ guarantees it. Spark generation is "largely random." — [GameWith](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/69695); [Goonhammer](https://www.goonhammer.com/212413-2)

### Inferences
- A consistent cross-game rule is a **ceiling on offspring above parents**: Rival Stars' foal grade ≤ parent grade, Howrse's GP fixed at birth, At Sevdalısı's existing `maxPotentialGainOverParents: 1.05`. Without one, repeated selection drives stats upward without bound (Photo Finish's Season 3 grade skew, below, is a symptom).
- Winning Post's model is the best template for "deep but not abusable":
  - The bonus (explosive power) is capped (halved above 25% blood share).
  - Risk scales with closeness of inbreeding.
  - Risk can be offset only by investing in healthy parents.
  - Nicks are a no-risk but harder-to-find alternative.

  This yields interesting choices rather than one dominant strategy.
- Hiding the true stats until retirement (Photo Finish) and selling previews (breeding reports) turns information into a sink and slows min-maxing. It also risks feeling opaque; Rival Stars' "Definite Breed Makeup" screen is the transparent counterpart.
- Umamusume's affinity multiplier (1 + affinity/100) and the halving at grandparent depth are a clean, testable formula for how much pedigree depth matters.
- A "guaranteed random trait slot" (Rival Stars) keeps every foal surprising even from identical parents. Under At Sevdalısı's determinism rule this must come from a seeded PRNG (e.g. `deriveRandom(seed, foalId, 'trait')`) with the seed hidden until birth, as the game already does for race seeds.

### Gaps
- No exact Rival Stars variance distribution (σ or range) was published in what I found.
- The Photo Finish gene list and dominance rules are not public in the extracts.
- No English-language confirmation that the Winning Post explosive-power numbers came from the developer; all numbers are community-measured.
- Derby Stallion 2 post-release mechanics are not verified.

## Q3. How breeding feeds the economy, and how games prevent oversupply/inflation

### Takeaway
Stud fees are the main player-to-player income in breeding games, and every surviving design takes a platform cut on them: 20–25% in Photo Finish, and a split where the stud owner gets only 10% in ZED Champions' 2025 model. ZED RUN is the cautionary case. Breeding was unlimited in total, nothing removed horses, and 420k+ horses were created. Horse values collapsed and the game shut down. The relaunch moved to hard generational slot limits and supply-neutral "breed by retiring the worst horse." Effective sinks seen in the field:
- retirement or lifespan
- sterilization with a bonus (Howrse geldings)
- an NPC buyback floor (Howrse Safe Haven)
- lifetime slot caps
- paid information (breeding reports)
- minimum stud fees

### Cited Findings
**Stud fees and cuts**
- Photo Finish: the stud owner sets the fee. The breeder pays that fee to the owner minus a 25% breeding fee (Beginner's Guide). The Season 3 primer instead describes a 20% service fee *added on top*, with a minimum stud fee of 4,000 DERBY (about $50). The two sources conflict, perhaps because the rate changed between seasons. — [Beginner's Guide to Breeding](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=5662); contradicted by [Season 3 primer](https://thirdtimegames.com/breeding-primer-season-3-breeding-opens-june-26/)
- Photo Finish: a retired horse can go to the Stud Market for a fixed fee *or* a percentage-based **Foal Share Agreement**, where the stud owner takes a share of the foal instead of cash. — [Third Time: Economy Deep Dive](https://thirdtime.medium.com/photo-finish-economy-deep-dive-66f033980485); [Investment Thesis](https://medium.com/@horse-racing-capital/photo-finish-live-investment-thesis-763919707ebb)
- Anecdote: a retired Photo Finish stud reportedly earned $35,000 in stud fees in one week. This is a promotional third-party claim, unverified. — [Jeff Opdyke on Medium](https://jeffopdyke.medium.com/photo-finish-live-the-web3-horse-racing-game-generating-tens-of-thousands-in-passive-income-84e0f2616e5f)
- ZED RUN (older): minimum breeding price = base 0.075 ETH, adjusted by the sire's bloodline. — [ZED RUN: Minimum Breeding Price and Allocation](https://medium.com/@zed_run/minimum-breeding-price-and-allocation-4946bbd1634e)
- ZED Champions (about mid-2025, X post with no exact date): updated Breeding Fee Allocation was 55% to internal balance, 10% to the stud owner's stake ("no more flat fee"), 20% to the player treasury and 15% to VHS (the company). — [ZED RACING on X](https://x.com/zed_champions/status/1943418307241197868)
- Howrse: covering fee set by the owner within a band of 500–7,500 Equus (a floor and a ceiling). — [Reproduction – Howrse fan site](https://howreses.page.tl/Reproduction.htm)
- Rival Stars: breeding cost (in Gold) is set by the highest grade and the stats of the pair. Players estimate about 400k Gold per bred foal. A desktop player saw a stallion cost about 9M Gold at level 1, and a forum post describes spending 100M Gold on a single tier-10 stallion. — [fan wiki](https://rival-star-horse-racing-player-guide.fandom.com/wiki/Breeding); [Steam guide: Making a Profit From Breeding Horses](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3539957796); [Aywren's Nook (Sept 2024)](https://aywren.com/2024/09/17/rival-stars-horse-racing-desktop-many-races-to-run/)

**Oversupply / inflation evidence**
- Naavik (Dec 2021) warned that ZED RUN's horse population would keep growing because horses could breed indefinitely and nothing removed them, devaluing horses and discouraging breeding. It also noted most players were expected to lose money racing. — [Naavik: ZED RUN – The Need for Greater Horsepower](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/)
- The same analysis observed that breeding-fee spend peaked in September 2021 and fell for two straight months, along with a steady drop in new births per month. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/dec-2021-zed-run/)
- Data40 lists breeding-driven asset inflation (excess horse production diluting value) as a core mistake. It says 420,000+ NFT horses were created and that the company stopped supporting ZED RUN after about six years. — [Data40: ZED RUN as an investment case](https://data40.com/articles/zed-run-as-an-investment-case-growth-economics-and-key-mistakes/) (secondary analysis)
- A 2023 community post argued "Breeding 3.0" devalued high-end breeding stock and saw Genesis floor prices fall. — [Queen Bean Racing: ZED RUN in 2023 and Beyond](https://medium.com/@queenbeanracing/zed-run-in-2023-and-beyond-8d68493e9d24) (opinion)
- After the original game shut down on 28 February (2025, per the article timeline), trading volume fell from about $28k (Feb) to $627 (Mar), and old horse NFTs listed at about $0.20. ZED Champions did *not* carry over horse NFTs. Badge holders got race and breeding horses in the new game. — [Cryptonews](https://cryptonews.net/news/gamefi/30875832/); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/316789/ethereum-nft-game-zed-run-reborn-base)
- Photo Finish Season 3: 1,311 fillies and 333 colts retired (the breeding pool), and "just under 800" of 1,302 foals were graded A+, A or A−. Most of a generation lands in the top grades, a sign of grade inflation through selection. — [Season 3 Breeding Report](https://photofinish.live/news/article/season-3-breeding-report)

**Sinks and supply controls seen**
- ZED Champions: lifetime breeding slots = 5 − generation; Buy Now breeding retires the lowest-ranked racing-pool horse (supply-neutral). — [ZED Champions Help: Breeding](https://help.zedchampions.com/articles/196584-breeding)
- Photo Finish: retirement is required to breed, horses have a lifespan, and studs stop breeding at age 24. — [Beginner's Guide](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=5662); [Investment Thesis](https://medium.com/@horse-racing-capital/photo-finish-live-investment-thesis-763919707ebb)
- Howrse: gelding (permanent sterility in exchange for a bonus); Safe Haven, where you sell a horse to the game for 500 Equus and it leaves the player economy; minimum sale price 500 Equus. — [Howrse Help Center: How can I sell a horse?](https://www.howrse.com/member/helpCenter/question?id=101); [Howrse fan wiki: Horse Sales](https://howrse.fandom.com/wiki/Horse_Sales)
- Rival Stars: limited foaling stalls (3) and Gold-heavy breeding costs; a foal's resale value is usually far below its breeding cost, so breeding is a Gold sink rather than a profit engine for most players. — [fan wiki: Foaling stall](https://rival-star-horse-racing-player-guide.fandom.com/wiki/Foaling_stall); [Steam guide: Making a Profit](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3539957796)
- Starters Orders: AI owners book your stallion and pay the fee but keep the foal, so stud income does not add horses to *your* stable. — [SO forum](https://www.startersorders.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?f=19&t=19485)

### Inferences
- Rough design matrix (synthesized from the above):

| Control | ZED RUN (old) | ZED Champions | Photo Finish | Howrse | Rival Stars |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lifetime cap per horse | none (monthly only) | 5 − gen slots | age 24 / lifespan | none (except donkeys 3) | not found |
| Rate cap | 3/mo stallion, 1/mo mare | slot per auction | days 8–14 per season | mare 10-mo rest | 3 stalls |
| Must retire to breed | no evidence | yes | yes | no | no evidence |
| Platform cut on stud fee | allocation model | stud owner only 10% | 20–25% | not found | n/a (NPC studs) |
| Supply removal | none (cause of inflation) | breeding retires worst horse | retirement/lifespan | Safe Haven, geldings | sell to NPC |

- Lesson from ZED: if each pair of horses can produce more than about 2 breeding-capable offspring over their lifetime and nothing removes horses, population grows geometrically. A cap like ZED Champions' (5 − generation) or an "each new foal retires one old horse" rule sets a hard ceiling.
- A platform cut on player-to-player stud fees acts as a sink and also deters wash trading between alt accounts. This matters for At Sevdalısı, where `anticheat.config.json` already flags multi-account value transfer; a stud fee paid between alt accounts is a value-transfer channel, like gifts and tribune gifts.
- A minimum stud fee (Photo Finish 4,000 DERBY, Howrse 500 Equus) blocks the "free stud to my alt" exploit; a maximum fee (Howrse 7,500) limits price manipulation.
- A Foal Share Agreement (stud owner gets a percentage of the foal's value or ownership) is a cash-less alternative, but implementing fractional ownership adds complexity. Under At Sevdalısı's ledger rule it would need ledger entries and locking design.

### Gaps
- No primary-company disclosure of ZED RUN horse population over time or player losses.
- Exact Howrse Equus inflation or horse-population data not found.
- Rival Stars: no official confirmation of how Gold breeding costs are calculated.
- The ZED Champions allocation post's exact date is not shown in the extract (it appears to be mid-2025).

## Q4. Market and auction systems for horses (fixed price vs auction, NPC sellers, price discovery)

### Takeaway
The mature pattern is several sale channels side by side: auction, fixed-price "direct sale," reserved sale to a named player, and an NPC buyback floor (Howrse). Stud services are increasingly auctioned too (ZED Champions' Stud Marketplace). Access to the trading channels is often gated by progression (Howrse objectives) to slow down new and alt accounts. Price discovery is anchored by floors: minimum sale price, minimum stud fee, formula-based minimum breeding price.

### Cited Findings
- Howrse sale types:
  - **Auction**: the seller sets a starting price and the highest bidder at the end wins. Duration is 3 days per one source and 48 hours per another (conflict).
  - **Direct sale**: fixed price, paid instantly.
  - **Reserved sale**: offered to one named player.
  - **Safe Haven**: sell to the game for 500 Equus.

  The minimum price is 500 Equus for every method. — [Howrse Help Center](https://www.howrse.com/member/helpCenter/question?id=101); [Howrse fan wiki: Horse Sales](https://howrse.fandom.com/wiki/Horse_Sales); [The Book of Howrse: Buying & Selling](http://thebookofhowrse.weebly.com/buying--selling.html)
- Howrse gates trading behind progress objectives: 50 objectives unlock direct sales and 100 unlock reserved sales (fan wiki; older sources cite seniority and karma thresholds instead, so the current rule is unclear). — [Howrse fan wiki: Objectives](https://howrse.fandom.com/wiki/Objectives)
- ZED Champions sells stud services by auction on a Stud Marketplace. Listing uses a breeding slot, which is refunded if the auction expires unsettled and lost if the owner cancels early. — [ZED Champions Help: Breeding](https://help.zedchampions.com/articles/196584-breeding)
- Photo Finish LIVE has an in-game marketplace for horses and a separate Stud Market (fixed fee or foal share). Third-party dashboards track average sale prices and horses due to retire next season. — [YSM Racing Club: First Time Buyers Guide](https://ysm-racing-club.medium.com/a-quick-start-first-time-buyers-guide-to-photo-finish-live-79d0a2bde5e9); [PhotoFinishEdge](https://photofinishedge.com/)
- Starters Orders 7 lets you put stallions up for offers from AI trainers and owners (an NPC demand side). — [Starters Orders 7 on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/978960/Starters_Orders_7_Horse_Racing/)
- Rival Stars sells horses for Gold (an NPC-priced sink). Community advice: never sell a foal or young adult right away, because the price is "always way under the breeding fee alone." An achievement exists for "Sell a horse worth more than 15,000 Gold." — [Steam guide: Making a Profit](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3539957796); [Steam achievements](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/1166860/achievements)
- Winning Post 10 has a dedicated "種付けとセール" (covering and sales) system in its official manual; sales likely mean yearling or mare auctions. The content was not readable. — [WP10 2026 manual](https://www.gamecity.ne.jp/manual/WP102026/jp/4700.html)

### Inferences
- Howrse's Safe Haven NPC buyback does two jobs: it sets a price floor and it removes excess horses. At Sevdalısı has no NPC buyback today; a low NPC buyback (well below the cost of breeding) would be a supply sink without becoming a money faucet.
- Gating player-to-player trade behind progression (Howrse) is a cheap multi-account defense and fits At Sevdalısı's existing level gates on breeds and equipment.
- "Slot refunded only if the auction expires naturally" (ZED Champions) is a small but clever anti-griefing rule: it punishes list-and-cancel manipulation without punishing honest no-sale listings.
- At Sevdalısı already has fixed-price and escrow auctions (CLAUDE.md (34)). The gaps against the reference games are reserved/named-buyer sales, stud-service listings and an NPC floor.

### Gaps
- No data found on auction durations or bid-increment rules for Photo Finish or ZED Champions.
- Winning Post's sale mechanics (NPC yearling auction pricing) were not retrievable.
- No source on how Rival Stars computes the NPC sale price.

## Q5. Player reception: what makes breeding satisfying vs grindy or exploitable

### Takeaway
Players reward breeding when outcomes feel *legible and steerable* but not deterministic. Winning Post and Derby Stallion communities build extensive theory guides because the hidden rules can be learned. Howrse players build calculators for BLUP and coat odds. Complaints cluster around three failures:
- variance so high that good parents produce bad foals (Rival Stars, until patch 1.44 narrowed it)
- breeding that costs more than the foal is worth (Rival Stars)
- unlimited supply destroying asset value (ZED RUN)

### Cited Findings
- Rival Stars players openly complain about breeding variance. Thread titles include "Breeding stats are terrible" and "Breeding Needs Serious Help!", and players report foals landing a couple of letter grades off the parent average. The developer responded with Update 1.44 so foals land closer to their parents' stats. — [Steam: Breeding stats are terrible](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1166860/discussions/0/4626979614631196639/); [Steam: Breeding Needs Serious Help](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1166860/discussions/0/4355619963541694791/); [Mobile Update 1.44](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/post/mobile-update-1-44-better-breeding)
- A Rival Stars fan wiki advises against paying Gold to breed, since "there are no guarantees the resulting foal [will] be a desirable color or good stats." Expensive randomness reads as a slot machine. — [fan wiki: Breeding](https://rival-star-horse-racing-player-guide.fandom.com/wiki/Breeding)
- A Rival Stars review credits inherited markings, track preferences and approximate coat as satisfying, while noting it is not an accurate genetics simulation. Partial realism is enough for most players. — [The Mane Quest (2020)](https://themanequest.com/blog/2020/6/21/review-rival-stars-horse-racing-desktop-edition-gorgeous-graphics-and-forgivable-flaws)
- Winning Post has a large community reverse-engineering its breeding theory across editions. A dedicated "verification group" (検証勢) on note.com publishes yearly 配合理論 write-ups for WP9 2020/2021/2022 and WP10 2025, which shows long-term engagement with a learnable hidden system. — [note.com 検証勢: WP10 2025](https://note.com/kensyouzei/n/n7928dc93006a); [WP9 2022](https://note.com/kensyouzei/n/n46d0e99c1295)
- Derby Stallion 2 guides focus on multi-generation "routes" (2nd and 3rd generation plans toward a "perfect" combination, e.g. "recreating Equinox"). Breeding is planned as a long project, not one-shot RNG. — [piyotakejapan: Epiphaneia route](https://piyotakejapan.com/47384); [piyotakejapan: Chateau Blanche](https://piyotakejapan.com/47419)
- Starters Orders players value the chance element ("a significant element of chance") but also note good AI mares are scarce. — [Operation Sports](https://www.operationsports.com/starters-orders-7-is-the-horse-racing-simulation-consoles-are-not-providing/); [SO forum](http://www.startersorders.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?f=19&t=20820)
- Umamusume's spark system is described as "largely random," and players farm runs for 3★ sparks. It is engaging but grindy, and the friend-rental economy softens the grind. — [Goonhammer: Understanding Sparks](https://www.goonhammer.com/212413-2); [games.gg](https://games.gg/umamusume-pretty-derby/guides/umamusume-pretty-derby-legacy-sparks/)
- Howrse players build external calculators for inborn skills and GP, and tools to estimate coat probabilities, a sign of a deep, numbers-driven but learnable system. Teams of up to 20 compete on a "best genetic potential" ranking, which makes breeding social. — [The Book of Howrse: Calculating Skills & GPs](https://thebookofhowrse.weebly.com/calculating-skills--gps.html); [Howrse App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id684830457)
- In ZED RUN, players and analysts attribute value collapse to oversupply from breeding and to rule changes (Breeding 3.0) that devalued existing breeding stock. Retroactive changes to breeding economics are seen as betrayal by asset holders. — [Queen Bean Racing (2023 opinion)](https://medium.com/@queenbeanracing/zed-run-in-2023-and-beyond-8d68493e9d24); [Data40](https://data40.com/articles/zed-run-as-an-investment-case-growth-economics-and-key-mistakes/)

### Inferences
- **What satisfies:**
  - A visible "expected outcome" screen (Rival Stars' Definite Breed Makeup, Photo Finish breeding reports).
  - Learnable hidden bonuses (Winning Post nicks, inbreeding).
  - A guaranteed small surprise (Rival Stars' random trait slot).
  - Multi-generation goals (Derby Stallion routes).
  - Inherited cosmetics (coat, markings).
  - Social lending (Umamusume rentals, Howrse stud coverings).
- **What frustrates:**
  - Wide stat variance on expensive pairings.
  - Resale below breeding cost.
  - Opaque hidden stats with no preview.
  - Retroactive nerfs to existing breeding stock.
- **Exploit vectors to design against (inferred from the mechanics above):**
  - Alt-account free studs (blocked by minimum fees and a platform cut).
  - Grade inflation through repeated selection (blocked by an offspring ceiling relative to parents).
  - Infinite horse farming (blocked by lifetime slots, retirement or lifespan).
  - List-and-cancel stud auctions (blocked by ZED Champions' slot rule).
  - Pre-computing foal outcomes from an open-source engine (blocked by a secret seed until birth; At Sevdalısı already uses this pattern for races).
- Changing breeding rules after launch should apply only to new pairings, never re-roll existing horses. Under At Sevdalısı's determinism rule (same seed + snapshot + config gives the same result), a config change would otherwise silently alter outcomes for already-committed breedings.

### Gaps
- No systematic review or survey data (only forum posts and wiki commentary) on breeding satisfaction for any of these games.
- No 2024–2026 reception data for Photo Finish LIVE breeding specifically.
- No reception data found for the Horse Racing Manager series.
