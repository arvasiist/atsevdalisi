# Progression, Onboarding & Retention in Horse Racing Management Games (and Genre Peers)

Researched 14.10.2026. **Method caveat (applies to everything below):** the session's egress proxy refused direct page fetches (Wikipedia, mobilegamer.biz, naavik.co, playday.one and thirdtimegames.com all returned CONNECT 403), so every finding comes from search-engine summaries of the linked pages, not from reading the pages in full. Numbers are reported as the summaries gave them. Spot-check them before quoting in a public document. "(older)" marks information from before 2024.

## 1. Onboarding: how the first 10–30 minutes are structured and how new players are guided

### Takeaway
The successful titles run a short, story-led or goal-led first session that puts the player into the core race loop at once and then opens features step by step through a level or rank gate. Rival Stars opens daily tasks and more events at player level 3. Umamusume opens Clubs only at a Team Trials rank. The genre-agnostic FTUE guidance is consistent on three points: deliver fun within 2–3 minutes, make the first interaction the best mechanic, and end every session with one goal done and the next goal visible. I found no horse-game-specific first-session drop-off data.

### Cited Findings
**Rival Stars Horse Racing (Foxie Ventures; mobile/PC/console/VR)**
- The framing narrative is restoring a family ranch. Racing is the main way to earn money. Once you "prestige enough", show jumping, cross-country and steeplechase unlock. — [Thumb Culture PS5 review](https://www.thumbculture.co.uk/rival-stars-horse-racing-ps5-review)
- Story goals sit in the top-left of the lobby, and daily goals sit in the same section. Upgrades unlock as the story progresses. Daily tasks and more events arrive at player level 3. — [TouchTapPlay guide](https://www.touchtapplay.com/rival-stars-horse-racing-cheats-tips-guide-to-build-the-best-ranch/)
- Horse levels run from 1 to 10 and gate which races are available. — [Champs of the Track](https://www.champsofthetrack.com/post/rival-stars-horse-racing-app)
- Completing story chapters and raising Prestige Level are the main lump-sum sources of free Gold (the premium currency). Silver is the earnable soft currency. — [TouchTapPlay](https://www.touchtapplay.com/?p=31864); [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=24226) (fan/affiliate guides; one source says login bonuses alternate Silver and Gold, another conflicts)
- Reviewers note that the early game involves "a fair amount of racing to afford upgrades". — [playday.one review, Apr 2026](https://playday.one/2026/04/27/rival-stars-horse-racing-review/)
- Reception: more than 4,600 Steam reviews at 92% positive after the PC release. — [Your Horse](https://www.yourhorse.co.uk/gear-advice/rival-stars-horse-racing-console-launch/)

**Umamusume: Pretty Derby (Cygames)**

**Genre-agnostic FTUE guidance**
- Let users "do something fun as soon as they open" the game. Do not open with a paywall, ads or forced account creation. Players should grasp why the game is worth their time within 2–3 minutes. — [GameAnalytics FTUE tips](https://gameanalytics.com/blog/tips-for-a-great-first-time-user-experience-ftue-in-f2p-games)
- The first thing players touch should be the most engaging mechanic. The FTUE must teach the rules and make the game seem worth the time. — [Unity: 10 FTUE tips](https://unity.com/how-to/10-first-time-user-experience-tips-games)
- Even the best games suffer their biggest losses on Day 1, and most players never return after Session 1. Unity recommends building a funnel over the tutorial steps and measuring completion rate and time-to-complete for early levels. — [Unity: retention at every lifecycle stage](https://unity.com/how-to/improve-retention-every-stage-player-lifecycle)
- Every session should end with a completed goal and a new one in mind, layered as short-, mid- and long-term goals. — [Adinmo FTUE](https://adinmo.com/first-time-user-experience-why-it-matters-and-how-to-get-it-right)
- devtodev's "New User Path" report maps new users' event sequences with time milestones to locate where friction causes drop-off. — [devtodev](https://devtodev.com/resources/articles/how-to-analyze-first-player-session)
- A GameAnalytics guest post by a mobile CEO argues that forcing early payment to reach content "you are likely going to lose half of your audience". This is opinion, not data. — [GameAnalytics: ten reasons why players quit](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/ten-reasons-why-players-quit)
- Supercell's CEO said the company spends much time on UI "to ensure they are easy to get into" (older). — [PocketGamer.biz interview](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/44543/1-7-and-30-day-retention-rates-are-key-says-tablet-first-supercell/)

### Inferences
- Common pattern: (1) a narrative or goal panel always visible in the lobby (Rival Stars' top-left story and daily goals), (2) a few early chapters that pay lump-sum premium currency, and (3) feature unlocks spaced by level or rank, so the menu grows with the player and does not open fully on minute one. A complex management game like Starters Orders adds a "simple mode" so new players are not overwhelmed. Horse Racing Manager's "clunky UI / not casual" verdict shows the cost of skipping this step.
- For At Sevdalısı: a keyword search of the repo (`onboarding|tutorial|loginStreak|hall of fame`, 14.10.2026) found no dedicated onboarding or tutorial system. Hits were only in auth/session and breeding files. The project already has ingredients a first-session script could chain: practice race, the player-controlled "drive it yourself" race, the `rookie-run` level bracket, Quick Join, quests and achievements. What is missing is an ordered first-session path ("feed, train, race, collect reward, join a calendar race") with a persistent "next step" panel.
- A scripted "guaranteed first win" (common in mobile) conflicts with the project's rule against hidden AI bonuses (CLAUDE.md §13.25). The compatible alternative is a transparent novice race against visibly weaker bots, using the existing `raceClassBotStatRange` / level-bracket bot ranges, so the first win is likely but honest.
- Following the GameAnalytics and Unity advice, the guest-first flow (the guest account exists) is the right default. Account creation, purchase prompts and the gem store should not interrupt the first 2–3 minutes.

### Gaps
- No published FTUE or tutorial walk-through for Rival Stars, Photo Finish or Umamusume describing minute-by-minute flow (first race, first win, first purchase prompt) was retrievable. A hands-on playthrough would be needed.
- No horse-game D1/D7 retention figures. The only generic benchmark found was "top-quartile D1 ≈ 26–28%, bottom ≈ 10–11.5%", attributed to GameAnalytics data via a vendor page ([iLogos](https://ilogos.biz/how-to-increase-mobile-game-revenue/)). It is unverified.
- I could not confirm when the first purchase prompt or starter pack appears in any of the target games.

## 2. Core daily loop, session length, energy/timer systems, daily/weekly quests, login rewards

### Takeaway
Umamusume is the clearest reference for a horse game. A daily checklist of five missions takes under 10 minutes. One heavy "Career" run is the main daily time sink. Capped regenerating stamina (TP 100 cap, 30 per Career; RP 5 cap, 1 per 2 hours) creates return reasons spread through the day. Hay Day and Top Eleven show weekly and 28-day cycles layered on top. Energy sold for money (Photo Finish's $0.99 recharge) is a monetization lever that players link to "pay to win".

### Cited Findings
**Umamusume**
- There are five daily missions, which can "easily" be completed within about 10 minutes of logging in. The Career playthrough is the main time-consuming daily mission and pays Jewels/Carats for the gacha. — [Deltia's Gaming: daily missions](https://deltiasgaming.com/umamusume-pretty-derby-all-daily-missions-explained/)
- The daily reset runs on Japanese Standard Time even in the global version, so the reset hour varies by region. — [Deltia's Gaming: daily reset](https://deltiasgaming.com/umamusume-pretty-derby-daily-reset-guide/)
- TP is the stamina for Career: 30 TP per Career, maximum 100. — [Deltia's Gaming: TP](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=280586)
- RP is the stamina for Team Trials: 1 RP per Team Trial, maximum 5, regenerating 1 RP every 120 minutes. Guides advise spending it often because excess is wasted at the cap. Matches auto-resolve quickly. — [Deltia's Gaming: RP](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=280536)
- A Career run: pick a scenario, a trainee, two "Veteran" Umamusume as inheritance parents (Sparks of Inspiration) and a six-card support deck. Most scenarios run 72 turns plus a 6-turn finale. — [umamusu.wiki Career Mode](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Career_Mode); a beginner guide says 70 turns ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/umamusume-pretty-derby-beginner-guide-tips-for-new-players)). The difference may be scenario- or version-specific.
- The Legend Race mode can be played three times daily. Star Piece rewards scale with performance against the featured "Legend" Uma. — [Deltia's Gaming: Legend Race](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279218); another guide describes ticket-gated attempts that regenerate on daily reset ([LootBar](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/umamusume-legend-races-guide.html)). The sources differ.

**Rival Stars Horse Racing**
- Daily tasks unlock at player level 3 and sit next to the story goals in the lobby. — [TouchTapPlay](https://www.touchtapplay.com/rival-stars-horse-racing-cheats-tips-guide-to-build-the-best-ranch/)
- Stall capacity is limited. Guides advise selling horses that consistently fail to place, so roster management is part of the routine. — [TouchTapPlay](https://www.touchtapplay.com/?p=31864)

**Photo Finish Horse Racing (Third Time Entertainment, mobile, 2015)**
- The primary loop is earning "bucks" by winning races and spending them to upgrade the horse or jockey. — [Game Developer: making of Photo Finish, part 1](https://gamedeveloper.com/audio/the-making-of-mobile-game-photo-finish-horse-racing-part-1)
- App Store IAPs include an "Energy Recharge" ($0.99) and an "Energy Bundle". The listing flags "Contains Loot Boxes". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id993764346)

**Howrse (Owlient/Ubisoft, browser)**
- Daily care means feeding, keeping energy high and watching morale. A horse's in-game day starts at 08:00 and it should be put to bed by 22:00. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id684830457); [community help page](https://elegantandeasydesigns-howrsehelp.yolasite.com/howrse-help.php)

**Top Eleven (Nordeus/Take-Two)**
- Seasons last 28 days. — [Nordeus official](https://nordeus.com/games/top-eleven/)
- Matches are scheduled at real dates and times, so managers watch live or come back for the result. An older review describes a two-day preparation period and 26 match days per season (older). — [Pocket Gamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/top-eleven/review/)
- Training drills (from relaxed to intensive) are set before fixtures. — [Pocket Gamer review (older, 2016)](https://www.pocketgamer.com/top-eleven/review/)
- May 2026: Nordeus signed with TyrAds to add rewarded "curated content" engagement, explicitly framed as retention that should "feel like part of the gameplay loop". — [Global Games Forum](https://globalgamesforum.com/news/tyrads-nordeus-announce-exclusive-partnership-to-drive-player-first-engagement-in-top-eleven)

**Hay Day (Supercell)**
- The plant, collect, sell and upgrade loop pays XP and coins at each step, and "promises progress for each return session" (older, 2014 blogger analysis). — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/game-monetization-design-analysis-of-hay-day)
- Supercell tracks 1-, 7- and 30-day retention as its key indicators (older). — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/44543/1-7-and-30-day-retention-rates-are-key-says-tablet-first-supercell/)

**Login rewards and passes (genre-agnostic)**
- Healthy login designs use cumulative counters with grace days and cosmetic rewards. Strict streak resets that punish a missed day, and power-gated rewards, are the burnout-risk version. — [Out of Games](https://outof.games/news/9618-how-live-service-games-keep-communities-engaged-between-major-updates/)
- A season pass works best when players already return regularly. It fails when rewards are weak, the grind is excessive, missions are disconnected from gameplay, or completion is unrealistic. — [Game Growth Advisor (2026)](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-31-liveops-strategy-mobile-games-guide/)

### Inferences
- Effective daily structure (Umamusume-style): a short checklist of about 5 items and about 10 minutes that returns premium currency, plus one optional deep activity of 20–40 minutes (my estimate; I found no source for actual Career run length) for players with time. Regenerating caps (TP/RP) spread check-ins across the day without demanding long sessions.
- At Sevdalısı already has daily and weekly quests derived from existing tables (CLAUDE.md (45)), horse energy and a readiness gate, care actions and training. The gaps against the reference pattern are: (a) a visible "today" checklist on the stable or home screen, (b) a login reward. If added, a login reward should be cumulative with grace days and cosmetic or soft-currency only, not a strict streak, and (c) a clear daily "deep" activity. The player-controlled race and calendar races are candidates.
- The project's gem policy (CLAUDE.md (81): no race-affecting item priced in gems) rules out Photo Finish-style paid energy refills. Energy must regenerate freely. This also protects against the top quit reason ("pay to win", §6).
- A fixed reset clock matters. Umamusume's JST reset is a known global irritant. The project's single game timezone (`quests.timezoneOffsetMinutes`, Turkey time) suits a Turkish audience, but the reset hour should appear in the UI.

### Gaps
- No source gave measured session length or sessions per day for any horse game.
- Rival Stars' energy or stamina mechanics (if any) and the contents of its daily task list were not found.
- Photo Finish's energy regeneration rate and race costs were not retrievable.
- I found no current (2024–2026) description of Top Eleven's daily match count or condition mechanics. Sources conflict on league size (14 vs 15) and promotion count.

## 3. Long-term goals: career milestones, legacy/hall of fame, trophies, achievements, ranks/leagues, seasons and resets

### Takeaway
The strongest long-term hook in the genre is generational: Umamusume's finished career trainees become "Veterans" whose inherited Sparks make the next run stronger, and Rival Stars, Starters Orders and Howrse center long-term play on breeding and lineage. This sits on top of relative-rank ladders that reset on a cycle: Umamusume's weekly Team Trials class promotion (Class 1–6, promotion if above class average), monthly Club ranks (D–SS), Top Eleven's 28-day seasons with promotion, and Photo Finish's named championships.

### Cited Findings
- Umamusume inheritance: every Career uses two Veteran Umamusume (previously trained horses) as parents for Sparks of Inspiration. — [umamusu.wiki](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Career_Mode)
- Guides recommend renting 3-star Sparks from friends early. Higher-star Sparks matter more than Spark compatibility. — [GameWith guide](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/69556) (attribution inside the search result set approximate)
- Umamusume Team Trials: everyone starts at Class 1. If your score beats the class average, you move up a class the following week, with Class 6 as the maximum. Scoring is points-based: skill activations and positioning add points, so the result is not purely stat-based. — [Deltia's Gaming: Team Trials](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281576); [Goonhammer](https://www.goonhammer.com/understanding-team-trials-in-umamusume-pretty-derby)
- Umamusume Clubs are ranked D to SS by fan count. Ranks are relative and recalculated at the start of each month. Rewards require at least 14 days of membership before the month begins. SS pays 3,000 Carats and 3,000 Club Points. — [Deltia's Gaming: Club Rankings](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=310487)
- As of September 2026 the mobile game has 135 playable Umamusume, a large collection goal. — [Wikipedia list of characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Umamusume:_Pretty_Derby_characters)
- Photo Finish mobile offers named championships: Breeder, Jockey and Cup Champion. Its progression runs "from a local derby all the way up to winning the quest for the Cup". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id993764346)
- Photo Finish LIVE seasons contain "thousands of races", from entry-level maiden races to premier graded races, a real-world-style class ladder. — [Third Time Games](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=4376)
- Rival Stars: horse breeding is based on real-life horse genetics. Breeds beyond Thoroughbred and Selle Français are hard to get without Gold "unless you're really consistent with the special events", so events feed collection goals. — [Equine on the Mind](https://equineonthemind.com/quite-possibly-the-best-horse-game-a-rival-stars-horse-racing-review-ios-version/)
- Rival Stars: prestige levels unlock disciplines (show jumping, cross-country, steeplechase). — [Thumb Culture](https://www.thumbculture.co.uk/rival-stars-horse-racing-ps5-review)
- Rival Stars Mobile Update 1.69 (Jan 2026) added separate Flat, Cross-Country and Steeplechase leaderboards alongside team leaderboards. — [Rival Stars official](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/post/mobile-update-1-69---leaderboard-update)
- Starters Orders 7 adds in-depth breeding and lineage stats, dynamic commentary and a betting-shop mode. SO6 covers stable building, breeding and riding as a jockey. — [Steam SO7](https://store.steampowered.com/app/978960/Starters_Orders_7_Horse_Racing/)
- Starters Orders Touch: train and breed a string and take on AI trainers worldwide "over realistic seasons". — [startersorders.com](https://www.startersorders.com/sot.html)
- Top Eleven: 28-day seasons. The top of the league is promoted to the next league (older). — [Nordeus](https://nordeus.com/games/top-eleven/); [Nordeus 2012 press release](https://nordeus.com/press-releases/nordeus-releases-new-version-top-eleven-facebooks-played-sports-game/)
- Hay Day: achievements include Derby-task milestones. — [Hay Day Wiki: Achievements](https://hayday.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements)
- Hay Day's team found it "had planned for enough content but in the end, it wasn't enough", a warning about endgame depth. The game reached 660 million downloads in ten years. — [MobileGamer.biz retrospective](https://mobilegamer.biz/what-supercells-hay-day-team-learned-from-ten-years-of-updates/)

### Inferences
- The "legacy" pattern (finished horse becomes a parent or permanent asset that improves future runs) turns each career into progress, not a dead end. At Sevdalısı has breeding, pedigree and inherited appearance. No keyword hit for a hall of fame or retirement-legacy system was found. A "Hall of Fame / retired champions" record, plus some mechanical carry-over through breeding, is the clearest missing long-term goal. It must be derived from existing race records, consistent with the project's "derive, don't count" approach for quests and seasons.
- Relative ladders matter for a small player base. Umamusume's "beat the class average to move up" and monthly relative club ranks avoid fixed thresholds that empty out. The project's seasons, weekly/monthly leaderboards and tier tournaments (min player level) exist. A division or class system (promotion/relegation between bands each week or season) is the missing layer that gives every player, not only the top of the leaderboard, a reachable rank goal.
- Umamusume's 14-day club-membership rule for monthly rewards is a cheap anti-club-hopping guard worth copying if club rewards are added (the project's club points already accrue from race XP, CLAUDE.md (15)).
- Hay Day's "not enough endgame" lesson argues for systemic goals (lineage, collection of 11 breeds × 5 hippodromes × race classes, ranks) over hand-made content, which a small team cannot keep up with.

### Gaps
- No source found for Rival Stars' prestige level count or end-game, or for any horse game's explicit "hall of fame" feature.
- No retention data tying breeding or legacy systems to measured retention. The link is inferred.
- Howrse's long-term goals (e.g. horse "Blups", competitions, the equestrian center) did not appear in search summaries.

## 4. Live ops: events, limited-time races, seasonal content cadence

### Takeaway
Live ops in 2024–2026 is dense and framed by seasons. GameRefinery measured a 35% rise in events in top F2P titles (May 2023 to Jan 2025), with midcore games averaging more than 20 concurrent events. In the horse niche the concrete examples are: Umamusume's recurring Legend Race and rotating Champions Meeting PvP over a constant daily Team Trials, Rival Stars' seasonal events (event-exclusive horses), Show Jumping Live Events and a global community challenge (10M rides), and Hay Day's weekly Derby.

### Cited Findings
- GameRefinery (Erno Kiiski, Sept 2025): events in top F2P games rose 35% between May 2023 and Jan 2025. Midcore titles run more than 20 concurrent events on average. Formats are shifting to overarching seasonal frameworks that unify missions, themed challenges and IP collabs. Seasonal albums, co-op events and minigame rotations are named as "the engines that sustain retention". — [Global Games Forum](https://www.globalgamesforum.com/news/the-trends-shaping-the-world-of-liveops)
- Umamusume: Champions Meetings (PvP) "come and go", while Team Trials stays a constant daily mode. — [Deltia's Gaming: all game modes](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279960)
- Umamusume Legend Race: a recurring event against a featured Legend horse that changes periodically. — [Deltia's Gaming](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=279218); [LootBar](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/umamusume-legend-races-guide.html)
- Umamusume global runs about 4.5 banners per month, an accelerated pace that leaves fewer free Carats between releases. — [TeraBox hub guide, Jul 2026](https://views.terabox.com/hub/how-to-plan-your-carats-for-the-global-uma-musume-banner-schedule) (low-quality source, treat as indicative)

### Inferences
- Two cadences are visible. A permanent backbone runs every day or week (Team Trials, Hay Day Derby), and rotating highlights run on top (Champions Meeting, Legend Race, seasonal horses, live events). At Sevdalısı's backbone is the race calendar (programs, featured `sunday-derby`, level-bracket runs), tournaments and seasons. Its rotating layer is admin-created `live_events`. Real-world tie-ins are a cheap, culturally strong hook. Photo Finish's Triple Crown timing worked. For a Turkish game: Gazi Koşusu (late June), Kurban/Ramazan bayramları and Cumhuriyet Bayramı.
- Rival Stars' community challenge is a low-cost event type. It needs only an aggregate count over existing activity, which fits the project's "derive progress from existing tables" pattern (quests, seasons) and needs no new counters.
- A "Legend Race" (PvE against a famous historical horse, a few tries a day, rewards scaled by margin) gives solo players a weekly target that does not depend on lobby population. It works without changing the race engine by using a fixed bot snapshot.
- Rival Stars' leaderboard-points mismatch (Update 1.71) shows that event leaderboards whose shown numbers disagree with credited numbers get pulled. The project's rule that reward order and displayed order come from the same function (CLAUDE.md (17)) is the right guard.

### Gaps
- No public cadence data (events per month) for Rival Stars, Photo Finish or Howrse.
- No Deconstructor of Fun, Liquid & Grit or GDC material on horse racing games surfaced. A search for a Deconstructor of Fun Umamusume deconstruction returned nothing.
- Champions Meeting schedule and rules for the global version were not covered by the summaries.

## 5. Social: clubs/guilds, friends, spectating, chat, gifting — which actually drive retention

### Takeaway
In the horse genre, social systems that retain are utility-based and cooperative, not chat-based. Umamusume Clubs have item donations with daily caps and points from friends borrowing your horse, and Friend Spark rental is a core mechanic. Hay Day's Derby is a weekly co-op neighborhood competition against 14 similar-size groups in 5 leagues. Rival Stars uses team leaderboards. Hard numbers showing guild members retain better than solo players were not found publicly.

### Cited Findings
- Umamusume Clubs: donating to members' item requests earns Club Points (5 per item, capped at 100 per day per one guide; another cites up to 10 donations per day, 2 items per person). Points also accrue when your "Star" Umamusume is rented or borrowed by others, under the same cap. The Club Point shop sells SSR support cards among others. — [Deltia's Gaming: club point items](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=281981); [GameWith](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/69676) (limits conflict between guides)
- Umamusume club rank (D–SS, relative, monthly, 14-day membership rule, SS = 3,000 Carats + 3,000 Club Points). — [Deltia's Gaming](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=310487)
- Friend utility: renting high-star Sparks from friends is standard early-game advice. — [GameWith](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/69556)
- Hay Day Derby: joining or creating a Neighborhood (from level 18) enters it automatically in the weekly Derby, Tuesday 08:00 UTC to Monday 08:00 UTC. Each neighborhood is matched with 14 others with a similar number of opted-in players and placed in one of 5 leagues, where higher leagues give rarer rewards. — [U7BUY guide](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/hay-day-understanding-the-derby/)
- Hay Day Derby tasks: a board of 12 tasks, with extra tasks purchasable with gems. Members' completed tasks add to the group's score. Rewards include vouchers, diamonds, items and coins. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/5-tips-completing-hayday-s-derby-event); the Derby launched in 2015 as a weekly-challenge feature (older) ([Gamenguide](https://www.gamenguide.com/articles/21857/20150312/hay-day-update-neighborhood-derby-adds-weekly-challenges-and-other-improvements-video.htm))
- Supercell contrasts Hay Day's cooperative gifting and trading with Clash of Clans' PvP (older). — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/44543/1-7-and-30-day-retention-rates-are-key-says-tablet-first-supercell/)
- Rival Stars: team leaderboards, and discipline leaderboards added Jan 2026. — [Rival Stars official](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/post/mobile-update-1-69---leaderboard-update)
- Third Time Games' older mobile Horse Racing Manager (since 2018) runs live races every 5 minutes in a world shared with other players. — [Third Time Games](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=330)
- Umamusume's real-world community: overseas fans donated to retired racehorses, which surprised the producer. Cygames asked fans to respect real ranches. The IP creates attachment to real horses. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/umamusume-boss-says-overseas-fans-shocked-him-with-donations-to-retired-racehorses-3387956/); [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/news/76040-umamusume-pretty-derby-developer-urges-fans-to-respect-racehorse-ranches)
- Evidence quality on guild retention: Bruin's "guild retention lift" pages are analysis templates (hypotheses like "does D30 rise for players who join a guild in week 1"), not results. — [Bruin](https://getbruin.com/use-cases/mobile-gaming/guild-feature-retention-lift/). An academic MMORPG study (Fu et al., 2017, *Decision Support Systems*) found that adding social features such as guild membership and role to segmentation improved player-lifetime prediction. — [DataLearner abstract](https://datalearner.com/academic/journal-papers/0167-9236/volumes-and-issues/263/paper-detail/4446)

### Inferences
- Pattern: social features with a mechanical reason to interact every day retain, such as donations that earn points, borrowing a friend's horse for inheritance, or a weekly co-op task board. Free-form chat alone gives no such reason. Matchmaking groups by similar size (Hay Day's 15-group brackets, 5 leagues) keep small groups competitive.
- At Sevdalısı has clubs, invites, club chat, club-only races, club points, friend-gift "send to all" with a daily collect limit, tribune gifts, emotes and live spectating. The missing pieces against the reference pattern: (a) a weekly club competition (Hay Day Derby-style) with a shared task board and size-matched brackets, (b) a mechanical friend utility, e.g. using a friend's stallion or jockey with a cooperative bonus, within the breeding and economy rules, and (c) relative monthly club ranks with a minimum-membership rule.
- The project's friend rule (friends only after a shared race, CLAUDE.md (64)) makes friend utility depend on race population. Bot-filled races do not create friendships. With low concurrency this can starve the social graph. Worth measuring.

### Gaps
- No public retention numbers for clubs, guilds, friends, spectating or chat in any horse game, or reliable cross-genre numbers. The common claim that guild members retain several times better could not be sourced.
- No evidence about whether spectating or live race viewing drives retention in horse games.
- Rival Stars' team or club feature mechanics (team size, joining rules) were not described in the summaries.

## 6. What players cite as reasons for quitting (grind, paywalls, lack of goals, empty multiplayer)

### Takeaway
The best quantitative source (Mistplay 2023, North America) puts "pay to win" first (59.75%), then bugs (55.18%) and "no sense of progress" (54.55%). Horse-game-specific complaints match: Umamusume (gacha and limit-break dependence, grind, RNG, time cost), Rival Stars (breed access gated by Gold), Howrse ("allegedly free" passes) and ZED RUN (money-losing majority under dominant stables, crypto friction, collapsing community).

### Cited Findings
- Mistplay 2023 North American survey: 59.75% stop because a game becomes "pay to win", 55.18% due to bugs or errors, 54.55% because they do not feel a sense of progress. Only 4.7% quit immediately after a bad update. Multi-select answers. — [GameDev Reports (Mistplay summary)](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/mistplay-north-american-players-behaviour)
- GameAnalytics: frustration and boredom are "the 2 most important" quit drivers (no percentages). — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/ten-reasons-why-players-quit)
- Nick Yee (MMORPG research, older c. 2003): the most common reason ex-players gave was boredom and repetition. — [Nick Yee, Daedalus Project](https://nickyee.com/daedalus/archives/print/000342.php)
- Umamusume global (player-review aggregator, 28 Jun 2025): common complaints are "predatory gacha mechanics", competitive progression tied to acquiring and limit-breaking support cards ("not recommended for F2P"), and "repetitive, grindy gameplay with excessive RNG". — [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/3224770/umamusume_pretty_derby) (early-launch sentiment only)
- Umamusume: heavy time investment inconvenienced casual and mid-level players. — [NamuWiki rating](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%9A%B0%EB%A7%88%EB%AC%B4%EC%8A%A4%EB%A9%94%20%ED%94%84%EB%A6%AC%ED%8B%B0%20%EB%8D%94%EB%B9%84/%ED%8F%89%EA%B0%80). In Korea, fan protests (Sept) against publisher Kakao Games over operation of the game led to an official meeting with fan representatives. — [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/uma-musume-fans-are-still-peeved-at-kakao-games/10475130)
- Umamusume's commercial success despite the complaints: about US$22M in the first month of global mobile (Sensor Tower estimate, iOS and Android only) — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/news/76527-umamusume-pretty-derby-gallops-to-us-22-million-in-first-month-of-global-mobile-release). Lifetime above $2.5bn, Japan 97% of lifetime mobile spend, $55.3M international spend over the prior three months — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/umamusume-pretty-derby-hits-25bn-after-most-lucrative-day-in-four-years). US: 32% of global mobile downloads, 29% of Steam players, more than 70% of mobile revenue (26 Jun – 25 Jul 2025) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/umamusume-en-mobile-steam-JP). Steam concurrent peak above 80k, popular with "core" players owning more than 100 Steam games — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/umamusume-pretty-derby-highly-popular-among-core-gamers-with-over-100-games-on-steam-research-says/). A NamuWiki claim of "50,000 average daily active users" is unsourced and unreliable ([NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%9A%B0%EB%A7%88%EB%AC%B4%EC%8A%A4%EB%A9%94%20%ED%94%84%EB%A6%AC%ED%8B%B0%20%EB%8D%94%EB%B9%84)).
- Rival Stars: without Gold, getting breeds beyond Thoroughbred and Selle Français is "pretty difficult" unless you are consistent with events. — [Equine on the Mind](https://equineonthemind.com/quite-possibly-the-best-horse-game-a-rival-stars-horse-racing-review-ios-version/). Early game needs a fair amount of racing to afford upgrades. — [playday.one](https://playday.one/2026/04/27/rival-stars-horse-racing-review/)
- Howrse: a common complaint is that it is "allegedly free", because players, especially minors, are expected to pay real money for special features and Passes. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/Howrse) (wiki, not a survey)
- Photo Finish mobile sells energy refills and contains loot boxes. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id993764346)
- ZED RUN (older analysis, Dec 2021): horses looked identical apart from color, which reduced character and collectibility. ### Inferences
- The three biggest quit drivers map onto existing At Sevdalısı guardrails and gaps:
  - "Pay to win": the gem policy (cosmetics and convenience only; no race-affecting gem prices; no gem to chip conversion) is a direct counter. Keep it, and say it publicly in the store.
  - "No sense of progress": XP, quests, achievements and seasons exist. Players still need one visible "what next" surface: next level reward, next unlockable breed or race class, next achievement, today's checklist. Rival Stars' always-on lobby goal panel is the reference.
  - Bugs: the 55% figure supports the project's CI and verification discipline.
- ZED RUN's failure modes are relevant because At Sevdalısı has paid lobby races: (a) a money-losing majority under dominant stables is mitigated by level brackets, breed classes, table rules with loser refunds and burned bot share, but the "bots burn their prize share" rule makes low-population lobbies a net loss for players (CLAUDE.md §13.26), the same "most players lose money" dynamic, (b) sameness of horses is mitigated by the appearance and personality systems, (c) currency-value-driven engagement is avoided because chips are not real money.
- "Empty multiplayer" did not appear in the sources as a named quit reason for these titles, but ZED RUN's "struggling community" and Third Time's shared-world live races every 5 minutes point to the same risk. Bot fill and calendar races keep races running. Async ranked modes (Team Trials-style) reduce dependence on simultaneous players.
- Umamusume shows that strong IP and attachment (real-horse history, fan donations) can carry heavy time and gacha friction commercially. A small web game without that IP cannot rely on it, so it must keep friction low.

### Gaps
- No Reddit or forum threads about quitting were retrievable (fetches blocked; searches returned guides and aggregators). Quit reasons specific to Rival Stars and Photo Finish are inferred from reviews, not from churn data.
- The Mistplay figures come from a secondary Substack summary. The original report was not reached.
- No data on "empty multiplayer" as a measured churn driver in horse games.
- Howrse's churn and current (2024–2026) player sentiment were not found.
